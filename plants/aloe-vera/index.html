Aloe Vera

Aloe vera

Aloe Vera is a succulent with thick, fleshy, serrated leaves full of soothing gel used in skin care and home remedies for minor burns. It thrives on neglect, needs little water and is ideal for pots and dry gardens.

Family
Asphodelaceae
Native to
Arabian Peninsula
Mature size
40–60 cm
Season
Yellow flower spikes in winter
Known for
Soothing leaf gel

Care guide

  • Water: Sparingly; allow to dry fully
  • Light: Bright light to full sun
  • Soil: Gritty, sharply draining
  • Difficulty: Very Easy
  • Growth: Slow
