Bhagwa Pomegranate

Punica granatum 'Bhagwa'

Bhagwa is India's leading pomegranate variety, with glossy saffron-red skin and soft, deep red, sweet arils. It is drought-tolerant, productive and well suited to hot, dry conditions.

Family
Lythraceae
Native to
Iran to northern India; variety from Maharashtra
Mature size
3–4 m tall
Season
Fruit through much of the year depending on pruning
Known for
Deep red, sweet arils

Care guide

  • Water: Regular during fruiting
  • Light: Full sun
  • Soil: Rich, well-draining loam
  • Difficulty: Moderate
  • Growth: Medium
