Surinam Poplar

Triplaris surinamensis

The Triplaris is a tall, upright tree that becomes a striking sight in bloom, with the female trees covered in rusty-red, winged fruit clusters. Its straight trunk and narrow shape suit avenues and boundaries.

Family
Polygonaceae
Native to
Northern South America
Mature size
15–20 m tall
Season
Flowers and fruits in spring
Known for
Crown turns rust-red with winged fruits

Care guide

  • Water: Moderate; deep watering once established
  • Light: Full sun
  • Soil: Well-draining loam
  • Difficulty: Easy
  • Growth: Medium to fast

Related plants

Uses

  • Roadside and boulevard planting

  • Shade over parking and walkways
