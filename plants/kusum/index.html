📖 About This Plant

Kusum is a large native tree whose new leaves emerge a brilliant crimson-red in spring, making the whole crown glow. Its seeds yield kusum oil and it is a traditional host tree for lac insects.

Family
Sapindaceae
Native to
Indian subcontinent and Southeast Asia
Mature size
15–20 m tall
Season
Flowers February–April
Known for
Blazing red new foliage

🌿 Where to Use It

  • Roadside and boulevard planting
  • Estate and campus avenues
  • Shade over parking and walkways
  • Institutional landscaping

💡  Care Tips & Growing Guide

Stake young trees until the trunk firms up
Prune lower branches to lift the canopy over traffic
Water deeply but less often to drive roots down
