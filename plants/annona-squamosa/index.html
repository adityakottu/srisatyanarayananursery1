Balanagar Custard Apple

Annona squamosa

Balanagar is a popular custard apple variety from Telangana, with large, knobbly fruits and creamy, very sweet white flesh. The plant is hardy, drought-tolerant and fruits within a few years.

Family
Annonaceae
Native to
Tropical Americas; variety from Telangana
Mature size
4–6 m tall
Season
Fruit August–November
Known for
Large, creamy, very sweet fruit

Care guide

  • Water: Regular during fruiting
  • Light: Full sun
  • Soil: Rich, well-draining loam
  • Difficulty: Moderate
  • Growth: Medium
