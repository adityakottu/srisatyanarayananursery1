Fiddle Leaf Fig

Ficus lyrata

The Fiddle Leaf Fig is a designer favourite for its huge, violin-shaped leaves with prominent veins. It creates a strong architectural statement in lobbies, living rooms and sheltered gardens, and grows best with bright, steady light and consistent watering.

Family
Moraceae
Native to
West and Central Africa
Mature size
2–3 m indoors; much taller outdoors
Season
Grown for foliage
Known for
Large, sculptural violin-shaped leaves

Care guide

  • Water: Moderate; deep watering once established
  • Light: Full sun
  • Soil: Well-draining loam
  • Difficulty: Easy
  • Growth: Medium to fast

Related plants

Uses

  • Roadside and boulevard planting
  • Estate and campus avenues

  • Institutional landscaping
