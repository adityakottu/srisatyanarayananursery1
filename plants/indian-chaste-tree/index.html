Indian Chaste Tree

Vitex altissima

A tall native tree with three-part leaves and clusters of small blue-violet flowers. It is hardy and valued in forestry and farm planting for its strong wood.

Family
Lamiaceae
Native to
Southern India, Sri Lanka and Southeast Asia
Mature size
15–25 m tall
Season
Flowers in spring
Known for
Native tree with blue-violet flower sprays

Care guide

  • Water: Moderate; deep watering once established
  • Light: Full sun
  • Soil: Well-draining loam
  • Difficulty: Easy
  • Growth: Medium to fast
