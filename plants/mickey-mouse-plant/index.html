Mickey Mouse Plant

Ochna serrulata

The Mickey Mouse Plant earns its name from black berries on bright red sepals that look like the cartoon's face. It also bears fragrant yellow flowers and is compact and easy.

Family
Ochnaceae
Native to
Southern Africa
Mature size
1–2 m
Season
Flowers in spring; berries after
Known for
Cartoon-like red-and-black fruit

Care guide

  • Water: Regular, allow surface to dry
  • Light: Full sun to part shade
  • Soil: Well-draining garden soil
  • Difficulty: Very Easy
  • Growth: Fast
