Sweet Flag

Acorus calamus

Sweet Flag (Acorus calamus) is grown and supplied by Gangumalla’s Sri Satyanarayana Nursery at Kadiyapulanka, Kadiyam. Available wholesale and retail in a range of sizes. Contact us for current stock, sizing and pricing.

Care guide

  • Water: Aquatic; keep submerged or saturated
  • Light: Full sun to part shade
  • Soil: Heavy loam or pond baskets
  • Difficulty: Easy
  • Growth: Fast
