Queen's Crape Myrtle

Lagerstroemia speciosa

Queen's Crape Myrtle, or Pride of India, bears large upright clusters of crinkled mauve-to-purple flowers in summer and the monsoon. Its smooth, mottled bark and leaves that redden before falling add interest in other seasons. The state flower of Maharashtra.

Family
Lythraceae
Native to
South and Southeast Asia
Mature size
10–15 m tall
Season
Flowers April–July
Known for
Showy mauve summer flowers
