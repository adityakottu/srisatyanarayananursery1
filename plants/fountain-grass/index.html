About this plant

Fountain Grass forms graceful arching clumps of narrow leaves topped by soft, feathery flower plumes that sway in the breeze. It is drought-tolerant and adds movement to modern landscapes.

Family
Poaceae
Native to
Africa and the Middle East
Mature size
0.6–1.2 m
Season
Plumes much of the year
Known for
Graceful swaying plumes

Best used for

  • Hedging and green screens
  • Borders and foundation planting
  • Topiary and formal shapes
  • Mass colour planting

Growing tips

  • Trim lightly and often for dense growth
  • Pinch tips early to encourage branching
  • Mulch to hold moisture and suppress weeds
