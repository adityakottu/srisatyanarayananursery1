Alexandrian Laurel

Calophyllum inophyllum

A handsome, salt-tolerant coastal tree with thick, glossy leaves finely striped by parallel veins and clusters of fragrant white flowers with yellow centres. It thrives in sandy and seaside soils and is widely planted for shade and windbreaks along coasts. Its seed oil (Punnai oil) has a long traditional history in South India.

Family
Calophyllaceae
Native to
Coastal tropical Asia, Africa and the Pacific
Mature size
10–20 m tall
Season
Flowers twice a year, mainly spring and autumn
Known for
Excellent coastal and salt-tolerant tree
