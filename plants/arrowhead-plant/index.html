Arrowhead Plant

Sagittaria sagittifolia

Arrowhead is a marginal plant with distinctive arrow-shaped leaves and white three-petalled flowers. It grows in shallow water and adds interesting leaf shape to ponds.

Family
Alismataceae
Native to
Europe and Asia
Mature size
40–90 cm
Season
Flowers in summer
Known for
Arrow-shaped leaves

Care guide

  • Water: Aquatic; keep submerged or saturated
  • Light: Full sun to part shade
  • Soil: Heavy loam or pond baskets
  • Difficulty: Easy
  • Growth: Fast

Related plants

Uses

  • Ornamental ponds and water features
