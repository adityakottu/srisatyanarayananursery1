Pink Trumpet Tree

Handroanthus heptaphyllus

The Pink Trumpet Tree bears large clusters of rose-pink trumpet flowers with yellow throats, typically before the new leaves appear. It is a tall, upright tree that gives a memorable spring display along avenues and in parks.

Family
Bignoniaceae
Native to
South America
Mature size
15–25 m tall
Season
Flowers in late winter to spring
Known for
Clouds of pink trumpets
