Oleander

Nerium oleander

Oleander, or Ganneru, is a tough evergreen shrub with narrow leaves and clusters of pink, red or white flowers almost all year. It thrives in heat, drought and poor soil and is widely used on highway medians. All parts are highly poisonous.

Family
Apocynaceae
Native to
Mediterranean to South Asia
Mature size
2–4 m
Season
Flowers all year
Known for
Tough median shrub; highly poisonous
