Copperpod

Peltophorum pterocarpum

Copperpod (Peltophorum pterocarpum) is grown and supplied by Gangumalla’s Sri Satyanarayana Nursery at Kadiyapulanka, Kadiyam. Available wholesale and retail in a range of sizes. Contact us for current stock, sizing and pricing.

Care guide

  • Water: Moderate; deep watering once established
  • Light: Full sun
  • Soil: Well-draining loam
  • Difficulty: Easy
  • Growth: Medium to fast
