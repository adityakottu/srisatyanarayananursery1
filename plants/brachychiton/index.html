Brachychiton Rupestris (Bottle Tree)

Brachychiton rupestris

One of our rarest imports — the iconic Bottle Tree with its dramatically swollen trunk stores water and creates an instantly recognizable silhouette. Available in six size options. A true collector's specimen.

Brachychiton Rupestris (Bottle Tree) (Brachychiton rupestris) is grown and supplied by Gangumalla’s Sri Satyanarayana Nursery at Kadiyapulanka, Kadiyam. Available wholesale and retail in a range of sizes. Contact us for current stock, sizing and pricing.

Care guide

  • Water: Very low — highly drought tolerant
  • Light: Full sun
  • Soil: Sandy, well-draining
  • Difficulty: Hard
  • Growth: Very slow

Uses

  • Rare collector's gardens
  • Luxury resort centrepieces
  • Museum and botanical garden displays
