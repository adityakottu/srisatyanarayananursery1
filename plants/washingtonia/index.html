Washingtonia Palm

Washingtonia robusta

Washingtonia palms with clear naked trunks up to 3.5m are available as single or double specimens. Their dramatic height and feathery crown make them ideal for grand avenue and entrance plantings.

Washingtonia Palm (Washingtonia robusta) is grown and supplied by Gangumalla’s Sri Satyanarayana Nursery at Kadiyapulanka, Kadiyam. Available wholesale and retail in a range of sizes. Contact us for current stock, sizing and pricing.

Care guide

  • Water: Low — drought tolerant
  • Light: Full sun
  • Soil: Sandy, well-draining
  • Difficulty: Very Easy
  • Growth: Fast

Uses

  • Avenue and boulevard plantings
  • Large estate entrances
  • Resort landscapes
