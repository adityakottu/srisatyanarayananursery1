Wandering Jew

Tradescantia zebrina

Wandering Jew has trailing stems of leaves striped silver and green on top and purple beneath. It is easy, fast-growing and beautiful in hanging baskets and shady beds.

Family
Commelinaceae
Native to
Mexico and Central America
Mature size
15 cm, trailing
Season
Small pink flowers
Known for
Silver-striped, purple-backed leaves
