Chorisia Speciosa (Silk Floss Tree)

Ceiba speciosa

The Silk Floss Tree is recognized by its thorny, bottle-shaped trunk and vivid seasonal blossoms in shades of pink and crimson. A rare ornamental specimen that brings dramatic character to any premium landscape. Imported.

Chorisia Speciosa (Silk Floss Tree) (Ceiba speciosa) is grown and supplied by Gangumalla’s Sri Satyanarayana Nursery at Kadiyapulanka, Kadiyam. Available wholesale and retail in a range of sizes. Contact us for current stock, sizing and pricing.

Care guide

  • Water: Low — drought tolerant once established
  • Light: Full sun
  • Soil: Well-draining
  • Difficulty: Moderate
  • Growth: Fast

Uses

  • Avenue and street planting
  • Statement garden focal points
  • Large estate grounds
