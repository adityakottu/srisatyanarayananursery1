Nolina

Beaucarnea recurvata

Often called the Ponytail Palm, this is not a true palm but a succulent with a swollen, water-storing base and long, narrow leaves cascading like a ponytail. It is very drought-tolerant and a favourite for pots and dry gardens.

Family
Asparagaceae
Native to
Mexico
Mature size
1–3 m in pots, slow
Season
Rarely flowers in cultivation
Known for
Swollen trunk; cascading leaves

Care guide

  • Water: Moderate; reduce in winter
  • Light: Full sun to bright shade
  • Soil: Sandy, free-draining
  • Difficulty: Easy
  • Growth: Slow to medium
