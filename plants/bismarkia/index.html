Bismarkia Nobilis

Bismarckia nobilis

Prized for its striking silver-blue fan fronds atop a clean, tall trunk — the Bismarkia Nobilis is one of the most architectural palms available. A true focal point for grand entrances and open lawns. Imported specimen.

Bismarkia Nobilis (Bismarckia nobilis) is grown and supplied by Gangumalla’s Sri Satyanarayana Nursery at Kadiyapulanka, Kadiyam. Available wholesale and retail in a range of sizes. Contact us for current stock, sizing and pricing.

Care guide

  • Water: Low — drought tolerant once established
  • Light: Full sun
  • Soil: Well-draining sandy soil
  • Difficulty: Easy
  • Growth: Slow

Uses

  • Grand entrance plantings
  • Resort and hotel landscapes
  • Open lawn focal points
  • Coastal gardens
