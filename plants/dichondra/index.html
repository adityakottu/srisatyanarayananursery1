Ground Cover

Dichondra

Dichondra repens

Dichondra forms a dense carpet of tiny, round, kidney-shaped leaves, used as a fine-textured lawn substitute or trailing from pots. Silver forms cascade beautifully from containers.

Family
Convolvulaceae
Native to
Tropics and subtropics
Mature size
3–5 cm
Season
Grown for foliage
Known for
Fine, button-leaf carpet

Care guide

  • Water: Regular until established
  • Light: Sun to part shade
  • Soil: Any well-draining soil
  • Difficulty: Very Easy
  • Growth: Fast spreading
