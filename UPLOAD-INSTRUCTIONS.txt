WHAT TO UPLOAD THIS TIME
========================

Upload everything in this folder to the repository, EXCEPT:

   data/site-content.js     -- deliberately NOT included; keep your live one
   data/version.json        -- deliberately NOT included; keep your live one

New or changed:
   index.html                 brand name, Kadiyapulanka labels, catalogue names
                              link to each plant's page, directory link,
                              photo fix for plants opened from a link
   admin.html                 photos published as image files, VIP fixes
   vip.html                   supply note, quick facts
   data/plants.js             unique tagline for every plant
   data/page-builder.js       new branded plant pages + plant directory
   plants/                    all pages rebuilt; plants/index.html is the new
                              A-Z Plant Directory
   media/                     NEW - your uploaded photos as image files
   sitemap.xml                directory added, duplicate pages left out
   tools/, README.md          updated

AFTER UPLOADING
  1  Open the admin on the device you normally publish from.
  2  Import & Export -> Publish Now. If it asks you to "Load live content",
     do that once, then Publish Now again.
  3  That publish moves every photo out of data/site-content.js into media/.
     The content file drops from about 8.5 MB to a few KB, so the site loads
     much faster, and plant pages show their photos.

  Photos added later are uploaded as files automatically on each publish.

LATER, WHEN MOVING TO THE MAIN DOMAIN
  Change baseUrl in data/site-config.js, upload it, press Publish Now.
  Every page, the directory, the sitemap and the home page follow automatically.
  Then submit https://<your-domain>/sitemap.xml in Google Search Console.
