WHAT TO UPLOAD THIS TIME
========================

Upload everything in this folder to the repository, EXCEPT:

   data/site-content.js     -- deliberately NOT included; keep your live one
   data/version.json        -- deliberately NOT included; keep your live one

New or changed:
   index.html                 enquiry form, home-page search details
   admin.html                 new publish engine, conflict protection
   data/site-config.js        NEW - the site's public address
   data/page-builder.js       NEW - shared page template
   data/plant-routes.js       6 new routes for your published custom plants
   plants/                    all pages rebuilt (better Google data, WhatsApp
                              previews); 6 new pages for custom plants
   sitemap.xml, robots.txt    NEW
   tools/                     updated build and release check
   README.md                  updated

AFTER UPLOADING
  1  Open the admin on the device you normally publish from.
  2  Import & Export -> the first Publish Now will pause and ask you to
     "Load live content". Do that once. It is the new protection working:
     this device has not synced with the live site under the new system yet.
  3  Publish Now. From then on it works as before.

  Do this on each device you publish from.

LATER, WHEN MOVING TO THE MAIN DOMAIN
  Change baseUrl in data/site-config.js, upload it, press Publish Now.
  Every page, the sitemap and the home page follow automatically.
