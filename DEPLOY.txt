HOW TO PUT THIS ON GITHUB
=========================

WHAT TO UPLOAD — all of it, folders included:

    index.html          the website
    admin.html          the admin panel
    vip.html            the VIP client portal
    data/plants.js      390 varieties in 8 categories
    images/             11 photographs

index.html looks for data/ and images/ sitting beside it.
Upload index.html on its own and no photos will appear.


STEPS
-----
1  Go to github.com/adityakottu/srisatyanarayananursery1
2  Click  Add file  >  Upload files
3  Drag the WHOLE nursery-site folder onto the page
   (GitHub keeps the folder structure automatically)
4  Scroll down, click  Commit changes
5  Wait about two minutes


YOUR LIVE ADDRESSES
-------------------
Website   https://adityakottu.github.io/srisatyanarayananursery1/
Admin     https://adityakottu.github.io/srisatyanarayananursery1/admin.html
VIP       https://adityakottu.github.io/srisatyanarayananursery1/vip.html?key=vip2024ssn


ABOUT YOUR PHOTOS
-----------------
Photos you add through the admin panel are saved in your
browser, not in these files. They show on the website when
you view it in the same browser.

To make them visible to everyone, open
  Admin > Publish & Share
and follow the steps there.

The images/ folder holds only the starting photos that ship
with the site so a first-time visitor sees a complete page.


UPDATING LATER
--------------
Changed a plant or added photos? You only need to re-upload
the file that changed. Usually that is just index.html.
